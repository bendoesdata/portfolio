Asteroid Tones is a live data sonification of asteroids passing Earth today. It pulls data from NASA's Near Earth Object API to create a unique audio-visual experience.

Here's how to understand the sonification:

  1. Low notes represent large asteroids, while higher notes represent smaller ones.
  2. Asteroids closer to Earth produce sounds more frequently than those farther away.

Each asteroid travels around the center (Earth) at a speed proportional to its velocity.
